## Intent: why this work exists

**Both integrations**
1. What problem is Phase 2 meant to solve, and for whom?
2. Why now? What is driving the timing: a client, a contract, an incident, an audit?
3. What happens if we do nothing?
4. How will we know it worked? What would we measure?
5. If we could deliver only one outcome, which would it be: speed, accuracy, lower risk, or less manual effort?

**Vendor master data**
6. What goes wrong today because of how supplier data is collected: slow onboarding, payment failures, audit findings, duplicates?
7. Who feels that pain most: suppliers, analysts, the client's accounts payable team, finance?
8. Why should SDC collect this rather than the VMS's own supplier onboarding?
9. Does SDC need to hold this data, or only get it to where it belongs?

**Rate validation**
10. What does a bad rate cost when it gets through: overbilling, invoice disputes, underpaid workers, audit findings?
11. Who is rate validation protecting: the client's budget, the supplier relationship, the worker, REDACTED's compliance position?
12. Is the goal catching errors, enforcing rate cards, or being able to prove compliance?
13. At which point in the lifecycle does catching a bad rate matter most?

## Process: how it happens today

**Vendor master data**
1. What starts a supplier setup: a program go-live, a mid-program addition, a client request?
2. Walk me through the last supplier you set up, from first contact to their first requisition.
3. How long did that take end to end, and how much of that time was active work?
4. At what point is a supplier considered "set up," and does every team agree?
5. How do you request supplier information, and how many versions of the intake form exist?
6. Which documents do you collect alongside the data?
7. What comes back incomplete or wrong most often?
8. Where does the data go after you receive it, and how many times is it re-keyed?
9. Who creates the vendor in the client's ERP, and how does the ERP vendor ID get back to you?
10. Who verifies bank details, and how?
11. Are tax and VAT IDs checked? Is sanctions screening done? By whom, with what tool, and where is the evidence kept?
12. Who approves a supplier into the program, and does the client have a say?
13. How do you chase missing information, and how many rounds does it usually take?
14. Where do setups get stuck longest, and why?
15. How do bank detail changes arrive, and how are they verified?
16. Have you ever received a suspicious or fraudulent change request?
17. Who tracks expiring documents, and what happens when one expires?
18. How are suppliers deactivated or removed from a program?
19. Where does supplier data end up living, and who can access it?
20. If I asked for a supplier's current bank details and their history, how would you find them?
21. Is the same supplier set up separately for each client program, and do the records ever disagree?
22. How does the process differ by country or supplier type: agency, sole trader, SOW vendor?
23. How many suppliers per program, at go-live and afterward?
24. How often do payments fail or invoices get rejected because of supplier data?

**Rate validation**
25. At what points are rates checked today: requisition, offer, work order, conversion, invoice audit?
26. Does the VMS already enforce rate cards anywhere?
27. Who does the checking, and with what access and authority?
28. Where does the rate card live? Can I see the actual file?
29. How do you know which rate card version applies to a given engagement?
30. Where are supplier markup caps written down?
31. Are rate card bill rates gross or net of the MSP fee?
32. Walk me through the last rate check you did. What did you look at first?
33. What makes you stop and look closer?
34. Show me one you rejected, and one that looked wrong but was fine.
35. How do you tell a typo from a real rate?
36. How are rate exceptions approved, and could you prove a specific one was approved?
37. What happens when a rate fails, and how long until it's resolved?
38. How many rates do you check per period, and what share fail?
39. What is the most common error?
40. Has a bad rate ever gotten through? How was it caught, and what did it cost?
41. Do you keep a personal spreadsheet, notes, or checklist for this?

**Both integrations**
42. What part of this would you most like to never do again?
43. What would you never want a machine to decide here?
44. Can I sit with you while you do a batch?

## Requirements: what must be true afterward

**Both integrations**
1. Who is the sponsor, and who settles trade-offs when stakeholders disagree?
2. Who signs off that it's done?
3. Which clients, programs, countries, and VMS platforms come first?
4. What is explicitly out of scope?
5. What is the smallest version that would still be valuable?
6. What volumes and processing times must it handle?
7. What audit evidence must exist afterward?
8. Is Workato a given? Are paid verification or data services in budget?
9. Which other teams or systems must change, and are they committed?
10. Who supports it after launch, and who maintains the reference data and configuration?
11. How often will the business rules change, and who changes them?

**Vendor master data**
12. Which system is the system of record for supplier identity: the client ERP, the VMS, or REDACTED?
13. Is this one-time onboarding, or ongoing maintenance of a living record?
14. Is a supplier collected once across clients or separately per program, and do client contracts allow sharing?
15. Which fields are required, and which downstream consumer needs each one?
16. What do the key terms mean: supplier vs. vendor, client vs. program, active, approved?
17. Who is responsible for verifying bank details, tax IDs, and sanctions status?
18. What approval must a bank detail change pass before it takes effect?
19. Must the account holder name, as the bank holds it, be captured separately from the legal entity name?
20. Has the privacy office been engaged? Is a DPIA needed? Are there residency or retention requirements?
21. Which supplier types and languages must be supported?
22. How will existing suppliers be migrated?
23. Does the program's funding model require us to hold banking data at all?

**Rate validation**
24. Who owns the rate rules and tolerances?
25. For each rule, what should a failure do: reject, warn, or route to an analyst?
26. How must rate exceptions be approved and recorded so they can be validated against?
27. Where will the reference data come from, and who keeps it current?
28. Should rates be compared gross or net of the MSP fee?
29. What standard working day applies per program for converting between daily and hourly rates?
30. Which legal floors are in scope to validate, and which are flagged for human review?
31. Who may see pay rates and supplier markups?
32. Which decisions must always stay with an analyst?
