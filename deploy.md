Below is the recipe set as it stands after folding in the connector changes. Three tables so the rows stay readable: identity and flow, contracts, then internals. Status reflects what was in the 29 Sep export set versus handoffs; correct me where it has moved since.

Every function resolves its target the same way: read `RTR_Pool` (`target_kind`, `target_environment`, `data_center`, `folder_id`, `project_id`) and `RTR_Secrets.dev_api_token`, then map those into the connector's `target_kind` / `workato_environment` / `target_data_center` / `target_api_token` fields. Functions never write `RTR_Pool.state`; only POOL-01 does.

Two things that cut across the table and are easy to lose:

**Target resolution lives in the pool row, not in recipe logic.** Adding `target_kind` and `target_environment` columns to `RTR_Pool` (alongside `deployment_mode`) means POOL-11a, 12, 13, 14 and 15 are one step configuration for TEST, PROD and every client. Only POOL-10 and POOL-11 keep a mode branch, and only because build/deploy endpoints are DEV-only in projects mode.

**Data masking** is needed on exactly four step types: any step mapping `target_api_token`, the `authorize_connections` step carrying JSON, the `ensure_api_key` step, and the POOL-01 steps that write `api_token` to `RTR_Secrets`.
